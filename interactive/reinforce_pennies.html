REINFORCE in matching pennies

Watch a learner discover the best response with REINFORCE, and see why baselines matter. Each line below is one independent learner. Pick a scenario to get started.

The gameBoth players flip a coin. The learner wins +1 if the coins match and loses 1 otherwise.
The opponentFlips heads with a fixed probability. Against 0.35, the best response is tails.
The updateAfter each flip: z ← z + η (R − b) (ea − π).
