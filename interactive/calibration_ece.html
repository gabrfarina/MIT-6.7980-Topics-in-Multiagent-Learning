This comparison is separate from the single-round controls above. Changing its settings requires a restart of both comparison histories. Seeded simulations illustrate these outcome sequences; they do not prove the private-randomness theorem or promise a decrease every round.

Long run guarantee. On a fixed grid, the history update rule has asymptotic expected ECE at most h/2 when Nature cannot see the current draw. One branch or one round can still increase ECE.

The square measures bucket mean calibration errors, not individual prediction loss. Source: Foster and Hart (2021), Section V, Theorem 12.
